Free Algo Trading Strategy Builder: What You Actually Get

AlgoPuzzle is free to start: you can build a complete algorithmic trading strategy and export it as a real MetaTrader 5, MetaTrader 4, or cTrader file without paying anything or creating an account. Free specifically means two exports per device, with no signup, no credit card, and no time limit on when you use them - the builder itself, with no cap on how much you design or test before exporting. Nothing about the free tier is a stripped-down demo: it's the same block-based builder, the same three platforms, and the same generated code a paid export produces. What isn't included is unlimited exports forever - past the first two, a single export is a one-time $2 payment, or $9.99 unlocks unlimited exports for 30 days. Both are one-time purchases, not a subscription running quietly in the background.

What "free" actually gets you

Every part of designing a strategy is unlimited from the start: connecting condition, action, and risk blocks, testing different layouts, saving and reloading drafts - none of that touches the free-export count. Only the act of exporting - generating the actual .mq5, .mq4, or .cs file - is metered, and you get two of those with zero setup. There's no watermark on the generated code, no missing risk options, no platform locked behind a paywall - a free export and a paid export come out of the same generator. See how to build your first algorithmic trading strategy for the actual five-step process the free tier is meant to cover.

Why it can be free to start

The free tier works the same way most no-code tools structure pricing: the cost of letting someone build and test is small, so it's given away, while the cost gets covered by traders who need more than two exports and pay for it directly - not by selling data or running ads. Strategy data itself never leaves your browser and your own exported file; there's no server-side database of strategies to monetize in the first place, so "free" doesn't come with a hidden trade-off on privacy.

What happens after the free exports

Once both free exports are used, the export button offers a single $2 credit or a $9.99 30-day pass - the free tier doesn't reset by clearing cookies or switching browsers on purpose, since it's tracked per device rather than per session. A single export credit stays anonymous, the same as the free tier; the 30-day pass is the one option that asks for a login, only because it has to follow your account across devices rather than staying tied to one.

Common questions

Is AlgoPuzzle actually free, or is there a catch?

It's genuinely free to start: build strategies and export up to 2 of them with no signup, no card, and no time limit on when you use them. Beyond that, exports are paid (one-time, not a subscription) - that's the entire model, nothing hidden behind it.

Do I need to create an account to use the free exports?

No. The builder and the first 2 exports work with no account at all, tracked to your device. An account is only needed if you later want the 30-day unlimited pass, since that entitlement follows you across devices.

What happens after I use my 2 free exports?

You can buy a single additional export for $2, or unlock unlimited exports for 30 days for $9.99. Both are one-time payments - there's no subscription running in the background to remember to cancel.

Is the free version missing any features compared to paid?

No - the free tier uses the exact same builder and generates the exact same MQL5, MQL4, or C# file a paid export would. The only thing that's limited is how many times you export, not what you're able to build.

Does AlgoPuzzle sell my data to make the free tier possible?

No. Strategy data stays in your own browser and your own exported file - there's no server-side database of user strategies to sell in the first place.
